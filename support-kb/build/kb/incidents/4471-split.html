The two problems

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so “7 April” becomes “4 July”. App, email, and the booking-v2 form, whose hint asks for the day first.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type the month first, as told, and the form reads the day first. Same symptom, different code: only the three tenants on booking-v3, only through the form.
Do not count by intake tag. intakeTag is guessed from the reporter's browser and disagrees with the cause in dozens of reports. The cause comes from the tenant and the channel (causes.ts).

What changes for the desk

  • parser (#1): fixed — fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.
  • hint (#2): won’t fix — the booking-v3 form is retired.
  • “Known” was true for one of two.
  • Merging on subject alone hides causes that share a symptom. Before marking a big ticket known, split it by cause with this table.
  • The one-day moves in the footnote may be a third bug (time zones). Check whether they still come in now that the parser fix is out.
